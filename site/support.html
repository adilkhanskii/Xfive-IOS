Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Open Xfive marketing, go to Profile, tap Settings, then Delete Account and confirm. Deletion is permanent.

How do I report or block someone?

Open the chat or profile menu, then choose Report or Block. Reports are reviewed by the moderation team within 24 hours.

How do purchases work?

The 1,000, 2,000 and 5,000 credit packs are one-time consumables and can be bought again whenever you choose. The verified badge is a separate optional monthly subscription. Purchase screens show the current price and applicable renewal terms.

Why does Xfive marketing ask for photos or microphone?

Photos are used when you select course media, chat or portfolio attachments, AI references, or save generated media. Microphone is used only when you record voice messages.
