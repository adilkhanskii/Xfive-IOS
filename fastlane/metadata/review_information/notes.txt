Xfive marketing. Version 1.1.20 build 271.
New in 1.1.20: a task in Hub can target several countries, cities and up to 3
categories ("All countries" / "All cities" by default); when credits are not
enough, AI tools show a "Top up balance" button that opens the in-app Store;
the first 1000 new accounts with a confirmed email get 200 free credits once
(one per device), granted by the server, no purchase involved. Build 270 also:
the chat input grows up to 5 lines; AI tools show "Cost: N credits" and, when
credits are not enough, "N credits needed"; the app owner (admin only, checked
by the server) can pause the new-user bonus or change its size and limit.
Build 271 also: the app owner (admin only, checked by the server) can grant
credits to an account found by email, nickname or name; every grant is logged.
This update keeps the reviewed 1.1.10 payment safeguards: the selected StoreKit
product is validated before any purchase success is reported and credit
confirmation messages stay explicit. This build adds a premium tier to AI
voiceover (Home -> Voice): lifelike voices with previews, "Voice by
description" and "Own voice" (cloning requires recording or uploading your own
voice and an explicit consent checkbox). In 1.1.19 a voice picked in "Voice by
description" is saved to "My voices" and stays selected. Premium is priced in credits by the server. The home screen no longer
shows the "Live products" slide;
prices and charging are unchanged. No Kaspi or external checkout is added; all paid content is
unlocked only with credits bought through In-App Purchase.

Profile -> Store offers repeatable consumable packs of 1000, 2000 and 5000
credits. Verified badge is a separate auto-renewable monthly subscription.
Prices are loaded from StoreKit. Cancelled, pending or unverified purchases
do not show success. Server verification and exact-once delivery remain in place.
A single lesson is priced by the server in credits and purchased atomically.

Use the supplied appreview@x5studio.app demo credentials. The review account
has a capped isolated Sandbox allowance to exercise the purchase flow.
Ordinary TestFlight accounts cannot receive spendable credits from Sandbox.
Production purchases require Apple's signed transaction verification.

App Tracking Transparency: the app now includes the Meta (Facebook) SDK to
measure installs, launches, registrations and purchases from our ads. The ATT
prompt is shown once, on the main screen after sign-in (never at launch). If
the user declines, no IDFA is collected; Meta then measures only through
Aggregated Event Measurement and SKAdNetwork. No email, name or phone number
is sent to Meta. App Privacy is updated accordingly.

Mainland China remains excluded from availability. Existing privacy disclosures,
AI capabilities, account deletion and portfolio moderation are unchanged.
Portfolio moderation uses OpenAI and voiceover uses MiniMax and ElevenLabs, as
disclosed in the privacy policy.
