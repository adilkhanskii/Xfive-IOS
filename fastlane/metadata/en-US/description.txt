Xfive marketing is a creator workspace for marketers, entrepreneurs and creative specialists.

Organize your portfolio, learn from courses, find specialists in Hub, create marketing media, post tasks and keep client conversations in one app.

KEY FEATURES
• AI Voice — turn text into Russian or multilingual speech through a protected server-side MiniMax route, then preview and save the generated MP3.
• AI Video — create 5- or 10-second vertical or horizontal videos with audio through the official BytePlus Seedance 2.0 Fast route. Completed MP4 files can be played, shared or saved to Photos.
• AI Studio — a server-verified workspace for MiniMax voiceover, Seedance video, Cinema, VFX, Presets and a private cloud gallery. Only tools ready to create a real result are displayed, and a request that cannot be accepted is never charged.
• Live Fruits — answer a short questionnaire, edit a three-scene storyboard, generate consistent frames and turn the story into a vertical video.
• Startup Chat — work through a business idea, audience, offer, demand test and next action with an AI mentor.
• Hub — browse specialists, filter tasks by the categories saved in your profile, post tasks and respond to client requests.
• Chats — direct messaging with text, photos and voice messages, plus search, replies, archive, mute, pin, report and block controls.
• Portfolio — show photos and videos in your public profile, with likes, comments and pinned work.
• CourseUP — watch creator and marketing lessons. Authorized developer accounts can publish and maintain courses.
• Profile — choose your role, edit public visibility, add social links and enable a verified badge.
• Sign in with Apple, Google or email.
• Russian, English and Kazakh interface.

WHO IT'S FOR
SMM managers, designers, photographers, video editors, copywriters, marketers, founders and creators who need AI tools, learning, client chats and a public work profile.

IN-APP PURCHASES
The Store offers optional one-time consumable packs of 1,000, 2,000 or 5,000 credits. Credits are added to the signed-in Xfive marketing account and can be used for credit-based creator features and courses. Each pack can be purchased again whenever the user chooses; credit packs are not subscriptions.

The verified badge is a separate optional monthly auto-renewable subscription. It does not include credits.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID at purchase confirmation. The verified badge subscription automatically renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings.

SAFETY
AI video and Live Fruits requests are screened automatically before provider submission. Flagged prompts are rejected before credits are reserved. Chats include report and block controls. Users can delete their account from Settings.

PRIVACY
Authentication, profiles, chats, tasks, courses, balances and portfolio items are stored through Supabase. AI tools send only the inputs needed for the requested feature from a protected Supabase Edge Function to the configured provider. AI media inputs and generated results use private storage and short-lived signed links. OpenAI is used for Startup Chat, storyboard creation and automatic moderation. AI Voice uses MiniMax Speech. AI Video, Cinema and VFX use the official BytePlus ModelArk Seedance route. Xfive marketing does not use third-party advertising trackers.
